The pathway

From first assessment to first paycheck.

The same three steps in Sokoto as in Lagos. What changes is the trade — matched to what local employers are actually hiring for.

01

Apply & assess

2 weeks · local intake centre

A skills and interest assessment matches each applicant to a trade with real, local employer demand — not a generic list.

02

Train

8–12 weeks · online training

Online Interactive Digital Skills Training Programme — Cyber Security Fundamentals, Social Media Management & Content, GIS and Drone Mapping for agriculture, Renewable Energy Digital Systems, and Digital Marketing.

03

Placed

Ongoing · 6-month check-in

Participants who successfully complete the programme will be eligible to be considered for direct employment opportunities with our partner employers, as well as formal apprenticeship placements and specialist digital skills roles, subject to employer requirements, candidate suitability, and the availability of vacancies. — followed up at six months.

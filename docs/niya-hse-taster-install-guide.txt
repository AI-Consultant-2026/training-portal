INTRODUCTION TO HSE FOR OIL & GAS: INSTALL GUIDE FOR THE NiYA PLATFORM ADMIN
Taster course by Paleon Training Limited
File: Paleon-HSE-Taster-SCORM12.zip (SCORM 1.2, about 30 KB)
Time needed: about 15 minutes, plus 10 minutes to test

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU START
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- You need an account that can create courses (site administrator, or a manager
  or editing teacher on the course).
- Keep the zip as it is. Do NOT unzip it: the platform reads it as one package.
- The course works on phones and laptops, and everything it needs (text,
  diagrams, quiz) is inside the zip. It loads nothing from outside sites.

What the course reports to your platform:
- Status: "incomplete" until the learner passes the final quiz, then "passed".
- Score: the learner's best quiz score, 0 to 100. Pass mark 70 (11 of 15).
- Bookmark: learners resume where they stopped.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LEARN.NIYA.GOV.NG (ROCKET LMS): WHICH ROUTE TO USE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
learn.niya.gov.ng appears to run on Rocket LMS (a Laravel course platform).
Rocket LMS only accepts SCORM packages when its SCORM plugin is installed
(sold in the Rocket LMS plugins bundle). Check: Admin panel > Settings or
Plugins, or start a new course in the instructor panel and see whether
"SCORM" appears as a course or content type. Menu names vary by version.

Route A: the SCORM plugin is installed
  1. Instructor panel > Courses > New.
  2. Choose the SCORM type, fill in the title (Introduction to HSE for Oil &
     Gas), category, thumbnail and description, and set the price to Free.
  3. Upload Paleon-HSE-Taster-SCORM12.zip unchanged and save.
  4. Submit the course for review, then approve it in the admin panel
     (Courses > Pending review).
  5. Run the test in Step 6 below with a test student account, then check
     the student's result in the course's reports.

Route B: no SCORM plugin (use NiYA's own course builder)
  Paleon Training supplies a content pack (Paleon-HSE-Taster-Content-Pack.zip):
  a PDF with the 8 lessons, practice answers and the 15-question final quiz
  (correct answers marked), plus the diagrams as PNG files. Part 1 of the PDF
  has the full build steps.
  1. Instructor panel > Courses > New > type "Course", Free.
  2. Add one chapter and 8 text lessons, pasting each lesson and its images.
  3. Add a quiz with the 15 questions, pass mark 70%, unlimited attempts,
     and certificate turned on for the quiz.
  4. Certificates: Admin panel > Certificates > Templates. Use NiYA's
     existing template; issue on passing the quiz or on course completion.
  5. Submit and approve as in Route A. Rocket LMS then records the quiz
     score, marks pass/fail and issues the certificate itself.

PART 1: MOODLE (versions 4.x and 5.x)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1. Create the course
  1. Go to Site administration > Courses > Add a new course.
  2. Course full name: Introduction to HSE for Oil & Gas
     Course short name: e.g. HSE-TASTER
  3. Course format: "Single activity", then Activity type: "SCORM package".
     (This makes the course open straight into the taster. If you prefer the
     normal course page, choose "Custom sections" instead.)
  4. Under Completion tracking, set Enable completion tracking to "Yes".
  5. Click "Save and display".

Step 2. Add the SCORM package
  If you chose "Single activity", Moodle now shows the SCORM settings page.
  Otherwise: turn on Edit mode, click "Add an activity or resource" in the
  first section and choose "SCORM package".
  1. Name: Introduction to HSE for Oil & Gas
  2. Package file: drag in Paleon-HSE-Taster-SCORM12.zip.

Step 3. Recommended settings (expand each section on the same page)
  Appearance
    - Display package: "Current window"
    - Student skip content structure page: "Always"
    - Display content structure on entry page: "No"
    - Display content structure in player: "Disabled"  (the course has its
      own menu)
    - Everything else: leave as default
  Grade
    - Grading method: "Highest grade"
    - Maximum grade: 100
  Attempts management
    - Number of attempts: "Unlimited attempts"
    - Attempts grading: "Highest attempt"
    - Force new attempt: "No"  (so learners resume where they stopped)
  Compatibility settings
    - Mastery score overrides status: "Yes"
  Completion conditions
    - Choose "Add requirements".
    - Tick "Receive a grade", then choose "Passing grade" under it.
    - Tick "Require status" and select "Passed".
    (On Moodle 4.0 to 4.2 the same options are called "Require grade",
    "Require passing grade" and "Require status".)
  Then click "Save and display".

  Moodle should open the activity without errors. If it shows an error about
  the package, check that the zip was not unzipped and re-zipped.

Step 4. Set the pass mark in the gradebook (for "Passing grade")
  1. Go to the course > Grades > Gradebook setup.
  2. Next to "Introduction to HSE for Oil & Gas", choose Edit > Edit grade item.
  3. Grade to pass: 70. Save.

Step 5. Add the certificate
  Use the certificate tool NiYA already uses. Common options are the free
  "Course certificate" (mod_coursecertificate) or "Custom certificate"
  (mod_customcert) plugins.
  1. In the course, Add an activity or resource > your certificate activity.
  2. Choose or design the template. We suggest the line
     "Content provided by Paleon Training Limited" if both logos are agreed.
  3. Under Restrict access, Add restriction > Activity completion:
     "Introduction to HSE for Oil & Gas" must be "marked complete" (or
     "complete with pass grade" if offered).
  4. Save. The certificate now unlocks automatically for learners who pass.
  (Alternatively, under Course completion, set the SCORM activity as the
  condition and issue the certificate on course completion.)

Step 6. Test before going live (please don't skip)
  Use a test student account, not "Switch role", so that results are saved.
  1. Log in as the test student and open the course.
  2. Check: the course opens, the diagrams show, and the top bar reads
     "Unit 1 of 8". There should be no "preview mode" banner (that banner
     means the platform's SCORM connection was not found).
  3. Go to Unit 7 (Final quiz). Answer badly and submit. The course says
     you need 11 to pass.
  4. Click "Exit activity", then reopen it: it should return you to Unit 7.
  5. Retake the quiz and pass (11 or more). Exit the activity.
  6. As admin, check:
     - Course > Grades: the test student shows the passing score.
     - The SCORM activity > Reports: status "passed".
     - The certificate is unlocked for the test student.
  Paleon Training ran this same test on Moodle 5.3 on 23 October 2026:
  a failed attempt saved as 53 / incomplete, the pass saved as 80 / passed,
  and the gradebook showed 80 out of 100.

Step 7. Open it to corps members
  Enrol learners as you do for other NiYA courses (self-enrolment, cohort
  sync, or your NYSC camp enrolment), and add the course to the NYSC course
  list.

Reports you can export later
  - SCORM activity > Reports: status and score per learner.
  - Grades > Export: scores for all learners (Excel/CSV).
  - Reports > Course completion / certificate reports: who has completed.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2: OTHER PLATFORMS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The steps are the same in principle: create a course, add a SCORM item,
upload the zip unchanged, set completion to "passed", and attach the
certificate to completion.
  - TalentLMS: Course > Add > SCORM | xAPI | cmi5 > upload the zip.
  - Canvas: install a SCORM tool from your Canvas admin, then add the package
    as an assignment.
  - LearnDash, Tutor LMS and other WordPress plugins: these need a SCORM
    add-on (e.g. "Tin Canny Reporting" for LearnDash, the Tutor LMS SCORM
    add-on). Install the add-on, then add the zip as a lesson.
  - Platforms with no SCORM support: Paleon Training will supply the course
    as text, images and a quiz file so you can rebuild it in your own course
    builder. The quiz is then marked by your platform's quiz tool.

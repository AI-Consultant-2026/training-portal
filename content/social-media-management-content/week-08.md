## Influencer Identification and Vetting

Influencer marketing means partnering with individuals who have built genuine, engaged audiences to promote a business, its products, or its services. This can range from major celebrities with millions of followers all the way down to **micro-influencers** — typically defined as having somewhere between roughly one thousand and one hundred thousand followers — who very often deliver dramatically better engagement rates and, importantly, far more affordable costs for smaller local businesses.

Identifying the right influencer starts with **relevance**: does this person's actual audience genuinely overlap with your target personas from week two. It continues with **engagement quality**: a smaller account with consistently high, genuine engagement is very often more valuable than a much larger account with low, unresponsive engagement, which can sometimes even indicate purchased or fake followers.

**Vetting** means doing real, careful due diligence before any partnership: reviewing past content for consistent quality and genuine authenticity, checking for any previous controversies that could create meaningful brand risk, and honestly assessing whether their existing personal content style and values genuinely align with your brand's own positioning.

## Partnership Negotiations and Contracts

Once you've identified strong potential partners, formal negotiation begins. Key elements to clearly agree on include deliverables — precisely how many posts, of what type, on which specific platforms; compensation — which might be direct payment, free products or services, or some combination of both; usage rights — can the business legally repost or repurpose the influencer's created content elsewhere, and for how long; and, importantly, disclosure requirements — in most jurisdictions, including Nigeria, paid partnerships legally must be clearly, honestly disclosed to the audience, typically through a specific hashtag or platform-provided partnership label.

A written contract, even a genuinely simple one, protects both parties by clearly documenting all of these specific terms and timelines, and I'd strongly encourage you to never rely purely on an informal, verbal agreement for any paid partnership.

## Influencer Content Guidelines

While influencer content should always feel authentic and genuinely in the creator's own voice, not scripted like a traditional advertisement, providing clear brand guidelines still helps ensure consistent quality and messaging: key talking points that genuinely must be included, specific things to clearly avoid saying, and any required visual brand elements. The best influencer partnerships strike a careful, deliberate balance — enough guidance to protect the brand's core messaging, while still leaving genuine creative freedom for the authentic voice that made that influencer's audience trust and engage with them in the first place.

## Video Marketing Strategies

Let's return to video, building directly on what we covered back in week four, now with a specific focus on strategic formats. **Live video** creates real-time engagement and a genuine, valuable sense of authenticity and immediacy. **Reels and Shorts** — the short-form formats on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube respectively — prioritize discovery through each platform's recommendation algorithm, often reaching people well beyond a business's existing follower base. **Long-form YouTube content** supports deeper storytelling and genuinely valuable, in-depth educational content, and benefits meaningfully from YouTube's function as a long-term search engine, discussed back in week one.

A mature video strategy deliberately uses a mix of these formats, matched to specific goals, rather than treating "video" as one single, undifferentiated content type.

## Video Content Production and Optimization

Building on the production basics from week four, **optimization** means adapting video content specifically for how each platform actually works: vertical framing for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts, since these are consumed almost entirely on mobile phones held vertically; enabled captions, since a very large share of video is watched with sound off, especially in public settings; and platform-specific video length, since what works well on YouTube frequently doesn't translate effectively to TikTok, and vice versa.

## Performance Tracking for Influencer Campaigns

Influencer campaigns need the same rigorous, disciplined measurement we covered last week in Module 5. Track reach and engagement generated specifically by the partnership, use unique tracking links or dedicated discount codes to directly attribute actual conversions, and compare the resulting cost per engagement or cost per conversion against your other marketing channels, including the paid advertising we covered back in Module 4.

# Lesson 2: Video Strategy & Capstone Preparation

Last lesson covered influencer partnerships. This lesson returns to video with a specific focus on strategic formats, covers how to track influencer campaign performance, and then spends real, dedicated time preparing you for your capstone project.

## Video Marketing Strategies

Let's return to video, building directly on what we covered back in Week 4, now with a specific focus on strategic formats. **Live video** creates real-time engagement and a genuine, valuable sense of authenticity and immediacy. **Reels and Shorts** — the short-form formats on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube respectively — prioritize discovery through each platform's recommendation algorithm, often reaching people well beyond a business's existing follower base. **Long-form YouTube content** supports deeper storytelling and genuinely valuable, in-depth educational content, and benefits meaningfully from YouTube's function as a long-term search engine, discussed back in Week 1.

A mature video strategy deliberately uses a mix of these formats, matched to specific goals, rather than treating "video" as one single, undifferentiated content type.

## Video Content Production and Optimization

Building on the production basics from Week 4, **optimization** means adapting video content specifically for how each platform actually works: vertical framing for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts, since these are consumed almost entirely on mobile phones held vertically; enabled captions, since a very large share of video is watched with sound off, especially in public settings; and platform-specific video length, since what works well on YouTube frequently doesn't translate effectively to TikTok, and vice versa.

## Performance Tracking for Influencer Campaigns

Influencer campaigns need the same rigorous, disciplined measurement we covered last week in Module 5. Track reach and engagement generated specifically by the partnership, use unique tracking links or dedicated discount codes to directly attribute actual conversions, and compare the resulting cost per engagement or cost per conversion against your other marketing channels, including the paid advertising we covered back in Module 4.

## Preparing Your Capstone Project

Now, let's talk about what's ahead. Your capstone project asks you to build a complete social media presence for a new tourism business right here in the State — hotels, cultural tours, food experiences — starting from zero existing followers.

I want you to treat this as one connected story, not eight disconnected weekly assignments. Your strategy document should draw directly on the audience personas and positioning work from Module 1. Your content package should apply the ideation, calendar planning, and visual and video production skills from Module 2. Your ad campaign plan should apply targeting and budgeting from Module 4. And your analytics dashboard should apply the reporting principles from Module 5.

For a genuinely new tourism business with zero existing followers, think carefully and specifically about which platforms actually make the most sense as a true starting priority — recall Week 1's core lesson: better to be excellent on two well-chosen platforms than mediocre across six. Consider realistically what a genuinely new account can accomplish organically in three months, and where a modest, well-targeted advertising budget could meaningfully accelerate that early growth.

## Bringing It Together

We've now covered the complete arc of professional social media management: platforms and strategy, content creation and storytelling, community management and brand building, paid advertising, analytics, and finally influencer and video strategy. Your capstone project is your genuine opportunity to demonstrate all of this as one coherent, professional body of work.

For your assignment, create a full video marketing strategy along with actual sample video content. For your practical exercise, identify three real potential influencer partners and develop a genuine, complete pitch for each one.

ITF

Reclaim the levy you're already paying

Registered employers contribute 1% of annual payroll to the Industrial Training Fund. Structured training that meets ITF criteria can reclaim up to 50% of that contribution — turning a compliance cost into a training budget.

Paleon provides the documentation your claim needs: syllabus outlines, attendance records, and completion certificates for every enrolled staff member.

50% reimbursement rate confirmed with the ITF. Criteria and rates are set by the ITF and can be revised — reconfirm with your area office for large or long-term commitments.

Beyond the ITF reclaim

The levy reclaim is the fastest way to justify the spend — these hold whether or not a claim is filed.

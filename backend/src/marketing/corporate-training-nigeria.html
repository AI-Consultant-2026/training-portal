Overview

A corporate training company built around your skills gap

Most staff training and development companies in Nigeria sell fixed courses. Paleon Training builds corporate training packages around the specific digital skills gap you're trying to close — then tracks each employee's progress against it, the same way we track our own students.

That matters because "digital skills training" means different things to different teams: an oil and gas operator training staff on GIS and drone mapping for pipeline monitoring has almost nothing in common with a bank training staff on cybersecurity awareness, even though both are, technically, workplace digital literacy training. We run both, along with AI, HSE and digital marketing, as corporate programmes delivered in-house or online, as self-paced staff courses, or as our intensive in-person executive programme in London for leadership teams.

Sector-aligned

Nigerian Content context and sector-specific training

For oil and gas employers, the Nigerian Oil and Gas Industry Content Development Act 2010 requires operators to develop the skills of their Nigerian workforce. Courses such as GIS & drone mapping and HSE cover relevant technical skill areas, but Paleon does not claim NCDMB accreditation, so confirm with NCDMB or your Nigerian Content team what counts toward your plan — see our dedicated guide to oil and gas careers and NCDMB training in Nigeria for the full picture of how that policy shapes hiring and training in the sector.

For HSE staff training specifically, see our dedicated HSE careers and training guide, covering the NUPRC/NESREA/NOSDRA regulatory framework and what staff-wide HSE training actually needs to include.

For banks and telecom operators, see our dedicated staff training guide for banking & telecoms covering cybersecurity, digital transformation, and fintech skills for banks, and technical, IT, and digital marketing skills for telecom staff.

For a more intensive, leadership-level format across oil & gas, banking, and telecoms, our executive training programme runs as a 5-day in-person intensive in London rather than a self-paced course.

Common questions

Corporate training in Nigeria: frequently asked questions

Q1

Which companies provide corporate training in Nigeria for AI and cybersecurity?

Paleon Training is a corporate training company serving employers across Nigeria. It runs three corporate programmes: AI Workflow Productivity & Process Transformation (AI training for employees), CyberSecure Workforce (cybersecurity awareness training for all staff) and the AI + Cybersecurity Professional Accelerator (for managers and professionals). It also offers five project-based digital skills courses for staff: Cyber Security Fundamentals, AI & Machine Learning Fundamentals, GIS & Drone Mapping, Digital Marketing and HSE Fundamentals.

Q2

Can you deliver training in-house at our office in Lagos, Abuja or Port Harcourt?

Yes. Corporate programmes in Nigeria are delivered in-house, live online or hybrid. We work with organisations in Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Warri, Ibadan, Kano and across Nigeria, and teams in several locations can join the same live online sessions.

Q3

Can we claim ITF reimbursement for staff training?

Yes, if your organisation pays the ITF training levy. Employers can reclaim up to 50% of eligible training costs from the Industrial Training Fund, subject to the ITF's requirements. We provide the attendance records and programme documentation your claim needs.

Q4

How much does corporate training cost in Nigeria?

Corporate programmes are quoted per organisation, based on team size, the programme and how it is delivered. Individual course places start from ₦100,000 per person (HSE Fundamentals) up to ₦300,000 (AI & Machine Learning Fundamentals). Email corporate@paleontraining.com with your team size and the skills you are training for, and we will send a proposal.

Q5

When can our team start?

AI Workflow Productivity and the AI + Cybersecurity Professional Accelerator are fully booked until January 2027; you can enquire now to reserve a place from January 2027. Ask us about current availability for CyberSecure Workforce and the self-paced staff courses.

Q6

Can corporate training packages be customised for our company?

Yes. Course mix, team size, examples and pacing are set up around your organisation and departments rather than sold as a single fixed bundle, including options to prepare your own internal L&D staff to help cascade the training further.

Q7

Does this training count toward Nigerian Content (NCDMB) obligations?

Paleon does not claim NCDMB accreditation or approval. Our courses cover technical skill areas, such as GIS and drone mapping, HSE and cybersecurity, that are relevant to the workforce-development and training obligations oil and gas operators have under the Nigerian Oil and Gas Industry Content Development Act 2010. Check with NCDMB or your Nigerian Content team whether a specific programme counts toward your plan.

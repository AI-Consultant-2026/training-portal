Overview

Most staff training and development companies in Nigeria sell fixed courses. Paleon Training builds corporate training packages around the specific digital skills gap you're trying to close — then tracks each employee's progress against it, the same way we track our own students.

That matters because "digital skills training" means different things to different teams: an oil and gas operator training staff on GIS and drone mapping for pipeline monitoring has almost nothing in common with a bank training staff on cybersecurity awareness, even though both are, technically, workplace digital literacy training. We run both, along with renewable energy and digital marketing, as either self-paced corporate e-learning or as our intensive in-person executive programme in London.

Common questions

Frequently asked

Q1

What is corporate training and why do companies in Nigeria need it?

Corporate training is structured, employer-sponsored upskilling for existing staff, as opposed to hiring new graduates with the skill already built. Demand is highest in digital and technical skill areas — cybersecurity, GIS and geospatial work, digital marketing, and renewable energy — where the pace of change outstrips what most staff learned on the job.

Q2

Can corporate training packages be customized for our company?

Yes. Course mix, team size, and pacing are set up around your organisation rather than sold as a single fixed bundle, including options to prepare your own internal L&D staff to help cascade the training further.

Q3

Is this training Nigerian Content (NCDMB) compliant?

Our GIS & drone mapping and renewable energy & digital systems courses are built around the technical skill areas Nigerian Content-compliant oil and gas employers are required to develop in their Nigerian workforce. See our oil and gas careers guide for more on how NCDMB shapes hiring and training in that sector.

Q4

How much does corporate training cost for a team?

It depends on team size and course mix, so we quote per organisation. Email corporate@paleontraining.com with your team size and the skill areas you're training for.

Whether it's a small team closing one specific skills gap or a company-wide digital literacy push, we'll build the package around what you're actually training for. Email corporate@paleontraining.com, or see the executive programme for a leadership-level format.

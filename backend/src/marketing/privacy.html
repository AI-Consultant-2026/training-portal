Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Paleon Training ("we", "us") collects and uses personal data when you use the Paleon Training website and learning platform (the "Service"). We are the data controller for the information described below.

  • Principal place of business / Nigerian operations: Nigeria — where Paleon Training will be registered and where its Nigerian training, customer and commercial operations will be conducted.
  • Technology and management support: United Kingdom — strategic management, software/web application development and technical support are provided by the UK-based team.
  • Market served: primarily Nigeria, with the potential to serve international clients and learners online.

Contact: hello@paleontraining.com. Postal correspondence for the UK-based team: 42 Okemore Gardens, Orpington, Kent BR5 3PH, United Kingdom.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and provide access to courses you've paid for;
  • to process and confirm payments;
  • to grade assignments, quizzes, and capstone projects, and track your progress;
  • to send you service emails, such as password resets and payment confirmations;
  • when you use an enquiry or Career Match form: to recommend a course, and to send you a short series of emails about that course and its free first lesson (currently a welcome email and up to three follow-ups over about 10 days). Our team may also contact you by email or, if you gave us your number, WhatsApp. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email, or by emailing hello@paleontraining.com;
  • to investigate and fix problems you report, and to reply to you;
  • to improve our courses using your feedback, and — only if you ticked the box agreeing to it — to quote your feedback on our website with your first name, last initial and course, or show your capstone project as an example of student work. You can withdraw this at any time by emailing hello@paleontraining.com;
  • to keep the Service secure — for example, detecting suspicious login activity;
  • to meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your course data for advertising.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process most of your data because it's necessary to perform our contract with you (providing the course you enrolled in) or to comply with a legal obligation (such as accounting records). Where we rely on legitimate interest — for example, security monitoring, or following up on a course enquiry you made — we balance that against your rights. Follow-up emails about your enquiry stop as soon as you unsubscribe or become a paying student.

5. Who we share it with

  • Payment processors, to process card payments;
  • Our hosting provider (Render), which runs the application and stores its database and uploaded files;
  • Cloudflare, which delivers our website and protects it from attacks (it processes technical data such as IP addresses);
  • Our email delivery provider (InMotion Hosting), to send transactional emails (password resets, receipts, notifications) and the follow-up emails described in section 3;
  • Google (Google Analytics 4), to measure how our website is used, as described in section 9;
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), our error-monitoring service, which receives technical details when something goes wrong on our website or in the training portal (such as the page, browser, device type and error message) so we can fix it. Error data is stored in the European Union;
  • Instructors and administrators at Paleon Training, to grade your work and manage your enrolment;
  • Our UK-based technology and management team, which develops and supports the Service and handles problem reports;
  • law enforcement or regulators, where we're legally required to.

6. International transfers

We serve learners primarily in Nigeria, and may serve international clients and learners online. Our technology and management support is provided from the United Kingdom, and some of our service providers (listed in section 5) process data in other countries, so your data may be processed outside Nigeria. Where we transfer personal data outside Nigeria, we use appropriate safeguards required by the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep your account and course data for as long as your account is active, and for 1 year after account closure to meet legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution needs. We keep enquiry and Career Match details while we may reasonably follow up on your enquiry, and delete them if you ask us to. If you unsubscribe, we stop emailing you and keep only a record that you opted out, so we don't contact you again. Problem reports are kept for as long as we need them to resolve the issue.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • request deletion of your data, subject to our legal obligations;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • request a copy of your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@paleontraining.com. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC), which enforces the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

9. Cookies and analytics

We use strictly necessary cookies/tokens to keep you logged in and to protect your account. These don't need your consent.

We also use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use our website (for example which pages are visited, and whether someone registered or contacted us), so we can improve it. Google Analytics only sets its cookies (_ga and _ga_*) if you click Accept in the banner shown on your first visit. If you decline or haven't chosen yet, it runs in "consent mode": no analytics cookies are set and Google receives only anonymous, cookieless signals. We never send your name, email address or phone number to Google Analytics, and we don't use advertising cookies or share data for advertising.

You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

10. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for adults seeking professional/vocational training and is not directed at children.

11. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and rate limiting on login attempts to resist brute-force attacks. No system is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.

13. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to hello@paleontraining.com.

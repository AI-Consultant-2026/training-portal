The walkthrough

11 steps, about three minutes

Step 1 of 11 · Get your code

Create your free account

Go to paleontraining.com/register and fill in your name, email, a password, your state, your status and the course you're most interested in. It's free: no fee and no course purchase needed to become an ambassador.

Leave Referral code empty, unless another ambassador invited you.

Step 2 of 11 · Get your code

Open Refer & earn

After you sign up you land in the portal. Click Refer & earn in the top menu, or Get your code → in the blue banner on your dashboard.

Next time, log in and go straight to paleontraining.com/refer/me.

Step 3 of 11 · Get your code

Your PLN code is created

The first time you open Refer & earn, your personal code is created automatically. You don't need to apply or wait.

Every code starts with PLN followed by six letters and numbers, for example PLN3QA96V. It's yours to keep and doesn't change.

Step 4 of 11 · Get your code

Copy it and tell us

Tap Copy. The button changes to Copied!

Then send your code, your name and your university (or NYSC state) to Paleon on WhatsApp at +234 707 714 9989. We'll welcome you to the programme.

Step 5 of 11 · Share it

Share your invite link

Use the WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X or Instagram buttons, or Copy invite link. Each one shares a link with your code built in.

When your friend opens your link, the sign-up form fills in your code and shows “Invited by” your name. If they type the code instead, they must enter it when they register. It can't be added afterwards.

Step 6 of 11 · Choose how you're paid

Pick airtime or data

Airtime

Choose Airtime under How you want to be paid. Each reward arrives as a {{REFERRER_REWARD}} top-up on your line.

Data

Choose Data. Each reward arrives as a data bundle on your network worth up to {{REFERRER_REWARD}}. Bundles come in fixed sizes, so we send the largest one that fits within {{REFERRER_REWARD}}, which may cost a little less.

You can change this at any time. It applies to every reward that hasn't been sent yet. (Course credit is the third option; this walkthrough covers airtime and data.)

Step 7 of 11 · Choose how you're paid

Save your phone number

Airtime and data go to the number in Phone number for airtime or data. Until you save one, you'll see “Add a number so we can send your airtime reward.”

Type your 11-digit Nigerian mobile number (for example 0803 555 0198), then tap Save. You'll see Saved. Check it carefully: airtime or data sent to a wrong number can't be recovered.

Moved your number to another network? Tell us on WhatsApp so we send it on the right network.

Step 8 of 11 · Track your friends

Your friend signs up

When a friend registers with your code, Friends invited and Signed up go up. In Your referrals they appear as “Joined — not paid yet”.

There's no reward yet. A referral only counts once your friend pays for a course.

Step 9 of 11 · Track your friends

Your friend pays

When your friend's first course payment is confirmed, you've earned your reward. You'll see Paid (rewarded) 1, You've earned {{REFERRER_REWARD}} and “{{REFERRER_REWARD}} awaiting payout”.

Your referral shows “Paid — reward earned”, with Awaiting payout in the Reward column. Your friend earns {{REFEREE_REWARD}} in airtime at the same moment.

Step 10 of 11 · Get paid

We send your airtime or data

Airtime

The Paleon team sends {{REFERRER_REWARD}} airtime to the number you saved, on your network. It arrives like any normal top-up, and your network sends its usual text.

Step 11 of 11 · Get paid

It shows as Paid

Once your reward has been sent, the “awaiting payout” note disappears and the Reward column says Paid.

If it says Paid but nothing arrived, message us on WhatsApp with your code. Then keep sharing: there's no limit on how many friends you can refer.

Questions

Common questions about getting paid

How do I get a PLN code?

Create a free account at paleontraining.com/register, then open Refer & earn (paleontraining.com/refer/me). Your code, starting with PLN, is created automatically the first time you open that page.

When do I get paid?

After your friend's first course payment is confirmed. The Paleon team then sends your {{REFERRER_REWARD}} as airtime or data to the number you saved. Allow a few working days.

What if I typed the wrong phone number?

Correct it on Refer & earn and tap Save before your reward is sent. Airtime or data that has already gone to a wrong number can't be recovered, so check it carefully.

How do you know my network?

We work it out from your number and check it before sending. If you've moved your number to a different network, tell us on WhatsApp so we send on the right one.

Is a data reward worth exactly {{REFERRER_REWARD}}?

Data bundles come in fixed sizes, so we send the largest bundle on your network that costs no more than {{REFERRER_REWARD}}. It may cost slightly less than {{REFERRER_REWARD}}. Choose Airtime if you want the full amount.

Can I switch between airtime and data?

Yes, at any time on Refer & earn. The change applies to every reward that hasn't been sent yet. Rewards already sent stay as they were.

What does my friend get?

{{REFEREE_REWARD}} in airtime once their first course payment is confirmed. It's sent to the number they save on their own Refer & earn page, so remind them to add it. It isn't a discount on the course fee.

I haven't saved a phone number. Do I lose my reward?

No. Your reward waits for you. Save your number on Refer & earn and we'll send it.

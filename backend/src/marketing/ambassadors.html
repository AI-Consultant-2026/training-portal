01 · The programme

What a Paleon ambassador does

You're the person on campus, in your NYSC set or in your group chats who points people to practical skills they can use, and who helps them try before they decide.

In practice that means three things:

Share

Share the free tools

The free 2-minute Career Match recommends a course, and the first lesson of every course is free to read with no account needed. Both are easy to share because they cost nothing.

Help

Help friends choose

Answer simple questions: which course fits Oil & Gas, Banking or Telecoms, how long it takes, that it's self-paced. For anything you're not sure about, send them to us on WhatsApp.

Refer

Refer the ones who are ready

When a friend decides to join, they sign up with your code. When their first course payment is confirmed, you earn {{REFERRER_REWARD}} and they earn a {{REFEREE_REWARD}} welcome reward.

02 · Who can join

Who we're looking for

  • Final-year students and recent graduates at any university or polytechnic in Nigeria.
  • NYSC corps members, especially CDS group leads and people active in their state's corps member groups.
  • Department and faculty reps, student union officers, and club leads (tech, business, engineering, geography, environmental and safety societies).
  • Current and past Paleon students, who can talk about the courses first-hand.

What it costs

Nothing. You don't need to buy a course to become an ambassador. You only need a free Paleon Training account, which is where your personal code lives.

The best ambassadors are trusted and helpful rather than loud. Someone who shares once in the right group and answers questions honestly will refer more people than someone who posts everywhere.

Most important

Your friend must sign up with your code. Your invite link (paleontraining.com/register?ref=YOURCODE) fills it in for them, or they can type it into the referral code box on the sign-up form. A code can't be added after they've registered.

We'll send you up to three personal links:

Sign-up

Use it when a friend is ready to join. This is the link that credits your reward.

Try

Your free-lesson link

Opens the first lesson of a course. Good for starting a conversation.

Match

Your Career Match link

Opens the free Career Match. Best for people who don't know which skill they want yet.

The free-lesson and Career Match links let us see how many people you send our way, but they don't carry your code into sign-up. When someone who used them decides to join, send them your invite link or remind them to type your code.

07 · How to share well

Where sharing works best

Status

Your WhatsApp Status

Low effort and it reaches everyone who has your number. Post once or twice a week with a different message each time.

Groups

Class, department and NYSC groups

Only where the group allows it, and at most once a week per group. Ask the admin first if you're not sure.

1-to-1

Direct messages

The highest-converting channel. Message friends you know are job-hunting or unsure what to do after school or NYSC.

In person

Conversations

After a lecture, at CDS, at a career event. Show the Career Match on your phone. It takes two minutes.

What to say, in one line

"There's a free 2-minute Career Match that tells you which skill fits Oil & Gas, Banking or Telecoms, and you can read the first lesson free before paying anything."

09 · Rules

Do this, not that

Do

  • Say "free first lesson" and "free Career Match".
  • Describe the friend's reward as a {{REFEREE_REWARD}} welcome reward after their first payment.
  • Share only where promotion is allowed, and respect group rules.
  • Send people to us on WhatsApp for questions you can't answer.
  • Be honest about what the courses are: practical, self-paced skills training.

Don't

  • Don't say "free course". Only the first lesson is free.
  • Don't call the welcome reward a discount or "{{REFEREE_REWARD}} off". It's paid after payment.
  • Don't promise a job, placement or salary. Paleon builds skills and employability; it doesn't guarantee a job.
  • Don't spam: no repeated posts in the same group, no mass-adding people to groups.
  • Don't refer yourself or create extra accounts. Self-referrals and duplicate accounts don't qualify.
  • Don't pay or bribe people to sign up, or collect course fees on Paleon's behalf. Friends always pay Paleon directly.

A referral counts once, on the friend's first confirmed course payment. Referrals that break these rules may not qualify. We may adjust or end the programme at any time. See the Refer & Earn page and our Terms.

13 · Questions

Frequently asked

Q1

Do I have to pay to become an ambassador?

No. You only need a free Paleon Training account. You don't have to buy a course.

Q2

How much do I earn?

{{REFERRER_REWARD}} for every friend who signs up with your code and pays for a course, with no limit. You choose airtime, data or course credit.

Q3

When do I get paid?

After your friend's first course payment is confirmed. The Paleon team issues rewards by hand, so allow a few working days for airtime or data.

Q4

What does my friend get?

A {{REFEREE_REWARD}} welcome reward (airtime, data or course credit) after their first course payment is confirmed. It isn't a discount on the fee.

Q5

My friend signed up without my code. Can it be added?

No. Codes are entered at registration, so make sure friends use your invite link or type your code when they sign up.

Q6

Does a friend who only tries the free lesson count?

Not for a reward. A referral qualifies only when the friend signs up with your code and their first course payment is confirmed. Free-lesson visits still help: many people try before they buy.

Q7

Can I refer myself or use a second account?

No. Self-referrals and duplicate accounts don't qualify.

Q8

Can I promise my friends a job?

No. Paleon Training builds practical skills and employability, but it doesn't guarantee a job, placement or salary. Please don't promise one.

Q9

Who do I contact with questions?

Message us on WhatsApp at +44 750 882 3495 or email hello@paleontraining.com. Account problems: Support.

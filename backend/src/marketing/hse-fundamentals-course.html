Overview

What is the HSE Fundamentals course?

In short

HSE Fundamentals is a beginner-level online course from Paleon Training: 8 days of curriculum and 16 lessons. Each lesson is written, illustrated and paired with a video, with assignments and quizzes along the way. The course ends in a capstone project. The course fee is ₦100,000.

Who it’s for

Beginners, including graduates, NYSC members and workers moving into site, field or HSE roles in oil & gas and industry. No prior HSE qualification is needed.

Curriculum

What you’ll learn

DayModule
Day 1Introduction to HSE in Oil & Gas
Core HSE concepts and HSE in Nigeria’s oil & gas industry.
Day 2Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment
Types of hazards on oil & gas sites; risk assessment methods (HIRA).
Day 3HSE Legal and Regulatory Framework
Nigerian oil & gas HSE regulations; international standards and guidance.
Day 4PPE and Safe Work Practices
PPE selection and use; permit to work, gas testing and contractor safety.
Day 5Emergency Preparedness and Response
Emergency response planning, journey management, fire safety and first aid basics.
Day 6Incident Investigation and Reporting
Incident classification and reporting; root cause analysis.
Day 7Environmental Management
Environmental impact, pollution control, waste management and oil spill response.
Day 8HSE Culture, Auditing, and Career Pathways
Safety culture and leadership; auditing, inspection and HSE careers.

How it’s taught

Practical, project-based learning

  • Illustrated lessons — every lesson has a diagram built for its specific concept.
  • A video for every lesson — read it, watch it, or both.
  • Assignments and quizzes to practise and check understanding as you go.
  • Capstone project: Site HSE Management Plan Capstone — an HSE management plan for an oil & gas or industrial site.
  • Certificate of completion when you finish the course.

For careers and the regulatory landscape, see HSE careers and training in Nigeria. Some HSE roles also ask for specific external certifications — see is NEBOSH IGC worth it?

Common questions

Frequently asked

Q1

Is HSE Fundamentals the same as a NEBOSH certificate?

No. HSE Fundamentals is Paleon’s own practical course. Some employers require specific external certifications such as NEBOSH; check the job description.

Q2

Does the course cover Nigerian regulations?

Yes. Day 3 covers the Nigerian oil & gas HSE regulatory framework alongside international standards and guidance.

Q3

What do I produce at the end?

A Site HSE Management Plan capstone, which is practical evidence you can discuss in interviews.

Q4

How is the course delivered?

Online, through the Paleon learning portal. Every lesson has written content with a custom illustration and a matching video, plus assignments and quizzes.

Q5

Does completing the course guarantee a job?

No. The course builds practical skills and a portfolio project; hiring decisions are made by employers.

Q6

Can I try the course before paying?

Yes. The first lesson of Day 1 is free to read and watch, with no account needed: go to paleontraining.com/preview/hse-fundamentals.

Q7

Is the course self-paced?

Yes. You can start as soon as your payment is confirmed and work through the lessons at your own pace.

Q8

How long do I keep access?

For life. Once your payment is confirmed you keep access to every lesson and video, with no expiry.

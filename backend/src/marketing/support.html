Support

How to report an issue

Something not working on the Paleon Training portal? Email us the details below, with a screenshot of the error, and our team will look into it.

Where to send it

All support-related issues must be sent to support@paleontraining.com.

support@paleontraining.com

Email us the information listed below and attach a screenshot of the error. The button opens your email app with the details already laid out for you to fill in.

Email support

What to include

Tell us these five things

Candidates must provide the following information in every support email, so we can reproduce the problem quickly.

01

Full name

Your first and last name.

02

Email

The email address you use for your Paleon Training account.

03

Phone number

A phone number we can reach you on.

04

Type of phone

Android or iPhone (iOS), and the version of your phone, for example iPhone, version 15.

05

Browser

Google Chrome. If you were using a different browser when the problem happened, tell us which one.

Also attach a screenshot of the error message (or the screen where the problem happened) and say what you were trying to do at the time, for example opening a course page or submitting an assignment. Never include your password in an email.

How to take a screenshot

On most phones

iPhone

Press the side button and the volume-up button together (on iPhones with a Home button, press the side or top button and the Home button together). The screenshot is saved to your Photos.

Android

Press the power button and the volume-down button together. The exact buttons vary a little between phone makes. The screenshot is saved to your gallery.

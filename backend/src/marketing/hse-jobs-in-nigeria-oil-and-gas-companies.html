HSE Jobs at Oil & Gas Companies in Nigeria

Last reviewed: 16 September 2026 · Reviewed by Paleon Training

Overview

HSE is one of the most consistently needed functions in oil & gas, by nature of the industry. This guide covers what the roles actually involve at the entry level.

Safety, health, and environmental compliance are core, ongoing functions at any oil & gas operation — not a nice-to-have. This guide focuses on the entry-level and support-level picture, honestly, without inventing hiring volumes.

Key takeaways

  • HSE is a core, ongoing function at oil & gas operations, not a peripheral one — safety-critical work by nature of the industry.
  • Entry-level and support HSE roles typically involve hazard identification, safety documentation, and supporting compliance processes.
  • NUPRC, NESREA, and NOSDRA are the main regulatory bodies relevant to HSE and environmental compliance in Nigeria's oil & gas sector.
  • A structured HSE Fundamentals course and a real project are a realistic starting point for entry-level and support roles.
  • More senior or specialized HSE roles typically require additional qualifications beyond an entry-level course.

What the roles involve

Entry-level HSE, honestly

At the entry and support level, HSE work typically involves hazard identification, safety documentation and reporting, and supporting compliance processes that keep an operation aligned with regulatory requirements — real, ongoing operational work, not administrative box-ticking.

NUPRC, NESREA, and NOSDRA are the main regulatory bodies relevant to HSE and environmental compliance in Nigeria's oil & gas sector — worth knowing by name if you're pursuing this path, though this guide doesn't restate their specific requirements; check each regulator's own published guidance directly.

More senior or specialized HSE roles — safety management, environmental compliance leadership — typically require additional qualifications and experience beyond an entry-level course. See the full HSE Careers guide for the complete picture, including the science-degree question and HSE-vs-safety-officer terminology.

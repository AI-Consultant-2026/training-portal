Where to look

Real, named targets worth researching

Named platforms, not fabricated contacts — you'll still need to find the actual editor/contact at each one before sending anything.

  • Forum — Nairaland's Jobs/Vacancies board, still one of the highest-traffic Nigerian career discussion spaces
  • Jobs platform — Jobberman's blog/resources section
  • Business press — BusinessDay, Nairametrics, TechCabal (careers/HR-adjacent coverage)
  • Social — LinkedIn groups for Nigerian HR/L&D professionals, and for oil & gas / banking / telecom sector professionals specifically
  • University — career services offices at universities with strong oil & gas, engineering, or business programmes (UNILAG, UI, Covenant, UNIPORT among others worth starting with, given Port Harcourt's role in the sector)

None of this is automatic — each email, post, and listing needs a human to send or submit it, and to actually follow up. Track what's been sent where so the same directory or contact doesn't get pitched twice.

03The Banking Opportunity

Nigeria's graduate labour market is genuinely crowded. The National Bureau of Statistics' Labour Force Survey put youth unemployment at 6.5% against 2.3% for older workers in Q2 2024 — a gap that lands hardest on exactly the young graduates and NYSC members competing for a limited number of bank graduate-trainee places.

Three of our six courses map directly onto skills relevant to Banking employers — Cyber Security Fundamentals, Digital Marketing, and Social Media Management & Content — reflecting how digitally-native customer engagement, fraud/security awareness, and online channels now sit alongside traditional banking skills. Different institutions and roles screen for different things, and we're careful to tell learners to check the specific job description rather than assume any course alone qualifies them for a given position — we do not claim banks specifically require Paleon courses.

Worth flagging directly to you, given your background: this is a genuinely crowded market for digital-skills training generally, and the honest differentiator we can offer is depth and credibility, not exclusivity. Your judgement on which of the above three courses banks actually value, and which we may be over- or under-selling, matters more to us than our own assumptions.

Why this matters

Most organisations don't need more software. They need software that fits.

Across Nigeria, important work still runs on spreadsheets, paper forms and busy WhatsApp groups. Data is re-typed, payments are chased by hand, and reports take days to pull together. Off-the-shelf tools built for other markets rarely fit: they assume fast broadband, card-only payments and staff who already know the system.

Paleon Training's consultancy builds the platform your organisation actually needs, around your processes, your people and Nigerian realities, then makes sure your team can run it long after we've gone.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does a web application cost?

It depends on what the platform needs to do. After a free discovery call we give you a written, fixed quote in naira for an agreed scope, paid against milestones, so there are no surprise invoices.

How long does it take?

As a guide, a focused portal or internal tool typically takes 6 to 10 weeks from discovery to launch, and a larger multi-user platform 3 to 6 months. You get a timeline with your quote and see working software every two weeks.

Who owns the code and the data?

You do. The source code, the data and every account (domain, hosting, payment gateway) are set up in your organisation's name. There is no lock-in.

Can the platform be hosted in Nigeria?

Yes. We advise on hosting that meets the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and your own policies, including Nigeria-based data centres where that is required.

Do you work with government procurement processes?

Yes. We can respond to requests for proposals, provide the documentation your procurement team needs, and structure the work in milestones that fit your budget cycle.

What happens after launch?

We train your staff to run the platform, hand over documentation, and offer a monthly support plan for fixes, security updates and improvements. You can also take support in-house.

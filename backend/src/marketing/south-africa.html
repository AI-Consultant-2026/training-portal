In short

Paleon Training teaches the practical digital skills employers ask about at interview. Each course is online and self-paced, with written lessons, illustrations and videos, and ends with a capstone project you can show an employer. You get a certificate of completion when you finish.

Paying

How paying works from South Africa

  1. Start with ₦20,000. It unlocks Days 1 and 2 of any course. Pay the balance whenever you’re ready, with no extra fees.
  2. Prices are in Naira. You pay by Visa or Mastercard on Paystack’s secure page, and your bank converts the amount to rand at its own rate.
  3. Check your card works online abroad. Some South African cards need international online payments switched on in your banking app first.
  4. Instant EFT and other local payment methods aren’t available yet. If your card is declined, message us on WhatsApp and we’ll help.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I take Paleon courses from South Africa?

Yes. Every course is online and self-paced, so you can learn from anywhere in South Africa on a phone or laptop.

How do I pay from South Africa?

Course prices are in Naira and you pay by Visa or Mastercard on Paystack's secure page. Your bank converts the amount to rand at its own rate. Make sure your card is enabled for international online payments. Instant EFT and other local payment methods aren't available yet.

Can I try a course before paying?

Yes. The first lesson of every course is free to read and watch, with no account and no payment.

Is the content written for South Africa?

The skills are the same across Africa, but many worked examples come from Nigerian industry, and HSE Fundamentals covers Nigerian regulations alongside international standards.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. You get a certificate of completion when you finish the course and its capstone project.

Does the course guarantee a job?

No. The course builds practical skills and a project you can show employers. Hiring decisions are made by employers.

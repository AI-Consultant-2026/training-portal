About Paleon Training

Paleon Training Limited is a Nigerian digital skills provider based in Warri, Delta State. Our operational and support team is based in the United Kingdom: it runs the learning platform, learner support, assessment marking and progress reporting. NiYA would work with a Nigerian company, delivered and reported to a consistent international standard.

00Summary

NiYA has achieved national reach. Its own August 2026 figures report 239,726 young people equipped with skills and more than 1.2 million certificates downloaded, and every NYSC corps member now meets NiYA at orientation camp. The next step is to turn that reach into jobs.

We assessed niya.gov.ng, the NYSC Corner, the course platform and NiYA Jobs on 22 October 2026. The main gap is the step from a short course certificate to a job in the sectors that hire graduates in large numbers. Training is short and general, outcomes are not tracked, and the jobs board has no live vacancies and no oil & gas, banking or telecoms employers.

We propose NiYA Sector Pathways: a structured route that takes a corps member from camp, through an assessed sector course and a capstone project, to a work-ready CV that shows a digital skill on top of their degree, in four sector-ready skills. These are Cyber Security, HSE (Health, Safety & Environment), GIS & Drone Mapping and Digital Marketing. The pathway has three parts, and the first costs the Ministry nothing.

01What NiYA has built

Any proposal should start from NiYA's strengths, and they are considerable:

  • Reach into NYSC. Every corps member completes a first NiYA course within two days at orientation camp, and a second before the end of the service year. No other skills platform in Nigeria reaches a whole graduate cohort like this.
  • "One Youth, Two Skills." A clear idea that young people need a second, marketable skill alongside their degree.
  • A full ecosystem. NiYA Academy, Community, Gigs, StartUp, Greenhouse and NiYA Jobs, with a Job Fair planned on the digital platform.
  • Sector partnerships already working. Examples include NDPC (Youth Data Protection Officers), NAICOM (1 Million Youths in Insurance) and Cascador (founder funding).
  • An open door to industry. NiYA invites industry experts and institutions to "transfer your knowledge" by contributing courses. This proposal answers that invitation.

07Our recommendations to NiYA
  1. Measure jobs, not only certificates. Add a simple 3- and 6-month tracer to the NYSC pathway and publish employment outcomes next to training numbers.
  2. Turn "Two Skills" into a pathway. Let the camp course be a sector taster and the second course a full, assessed course in the same sector, so the two skills build on each other.
  3. Bring the big recruiters onto NiYA Jobs. Prioritise oil & gas, banking and telecoms employers and their contractors, and make sure the jobs board never shows an empty page; post curated roles while it grows.
  4. Make evidence visible. Let corps members upload their work-ready CVs, capstone projects and training records to their NiYA profiles, so employers see what they can do, not just a certificate.
  5. Hold a sector day at the NiYA Job Fair. Bring trained corps members and sector employers together on the platform.
  6. Fix the trust details. Repair the partner links, remove the plug-in footer credit, populate or hide empty pages, and use verifiable success stories with consent.
  7. Start small, then scale. Run Part B with 200 corps members in one batch and judge it on the final report before national rollout.

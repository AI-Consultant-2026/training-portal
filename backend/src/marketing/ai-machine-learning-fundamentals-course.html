Overview

What is the AI & Machine Learning Fundamentals course?

In short

AI & Machine Learning Fundamentals is a beginner-level online course from Paleon Training: 18 days of curriculum and 36 lessons. Each lesson is written in plain English and paired with a video, with a quiz and a hands-on assignment every day. The course ends in a capstone AI project. The course fee is ₦300,000.

Who it’s for

Graduates, NYSC members, students and working professionals from any background who want to understand AI and use it at work, whether you are aiming for a data or AI role or want AI skills in banking, telecoms, oil & gas, business or the public sector. No coding, maths or AI background is needed.

Curriculum

What you’ll learn

DayModule
Day 1Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
What AI is and is not, a short history, and AI vs traditional software.
Day 2Understanding Machine Learning
How computers learn from examples; supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning.
Day 3Understanding Data
Types of data, data quality, bias and privacy, with hands-on spreadsheet cleaning.
Day 4Python Basics for AI
Your first Python in Google Colab: variables, lists, loops, functions and reading data.
Day 5Data Analysis and Visualisation
Filtering, grouping and statistics in pandas; clear charts with Matplotlib.
Day 6Supervised Learning
Regression and classification; building a prediction model with scikit-learn.
Day 7Unsupervised Learning
Clustering with K-means, customer segmentation and anomaly detection.
Day 8Model Evaluation
Accuracy, precision, recall, overfitting, cross-validation and fairness checks.
Day 9Introduction to Generative AI
How large language models work; assistants, agents, RAG and their limits.
Day 10Prompt Engineering
The six parts of a good prompt, few-shot prompting, testing and AI-assisted research.
Day 11AI Automation
Triggers, APIs and no-code tools; an AI workflow with a human in the loop.
Day 12Introduction to Deep Learning
Neural networks, layers and weights; transfer learning for images and speech.
Day 13Natural Language Processing
Sentiment analysis, entity extraction, text classification and chatbots.
Day 14Computer Vision
Image classification, object detection, OCR and document processing.
Day 15AI Applications Across Industries
AI in mining, energy, agriculture, banking, healthcare and government; judging an AI idea.
Day 16AI Ethics and Responsible AI
Bias, privacy, transparency, deepfakes, copyright and AI governance.
Day 17AI and Cyber Security
Prompt injection, data poisoning, AI-powered phishing and deepfake fraud.
Day 18Capstone Project
Plan, build, evaluate and present your own AI project.

How it’s taught

Practical, project-based learning

  • Plain-English lessons — every idea starts with an everyday example before any technical word appears.
  • A video for every lesson — read it, watch it, or both.
  • Hands-on Python in Google Colab, a free online notebook — nothing to install.
  • A quiz and an assignment every day: clean a dataset, build a prediction model, segment customers, write a prompt library, design an AI workflow and more.
  • Capstone project: plan, build, evaluate and present your own AI project, choosing from eight options including predictive analytics, a customer-service assistant, automation, and AI for mining, agriculture, energy or education.
  • Responsible and secure AI — bias, privacy, deepfakes, copyright, prompt injection and AI-powered fraud.
  • Certificate of completion when you finish the course.
  • CV and reference support when you finish the course and your capstone.

Common questions

Frequently asked

Q1

Do I need coding or maths experience?

No. Every idea is explained in plain English with an everyday example first. You write your first Python on Day 4, one small step at a time, in Google Colab, a free online notebook that runs in your browser.

Q2

What equipment do I need?

A laptop or desktop with a web browser and an internet connection, plus a free Google account for Colab. You can read and watch lessons on your phone, but the hands-on Python work is easier on a computer.

Q3

Which tools will I use?

Google Colab, Python, pandas, Matplotlib and scikit-learn for data and machine learning; generative AI assistants for prompting; a no-code automation tool such as Zapier or Make; and Teachable Machine or Hugging Face for deep learning. All have free options.

Q4

What do I produce at the end?

A capstone AI project you choose from eight options (for example a predictive model, a customer-service assistant, an automation workflow, or AI for mining, agriculture, energy or education), plus a daily assignment for each of the other days. Together they make a practical portfolio you can show employers.

Q5

Do you help with CVs and job applications?

Yes. Once you’ve completed the course and your capstone project, we help you prepare a CV that presents your skills and your capstone as practical evidence for employers. We also provide a reference from Paleon Training confirming the course and capstone you completed. Email hello@paleontraining.com to request them.

Q6

Is this a recognised AI certification?

No. You receive a Paleon certificate of completion. It is not a university degree or a vendor certification from Google, Microsoft or AWS, though the course is a practical foundation for those.

Q7

Does completing the course guarantee a job?

No. The course builds practical skills and a portfolio, and we help with your CV and a reference once you finish. Hiring decisions are made by employers.

Q8

Can I try the course before paying?

Yes. The first lesson of Day 1 is free to read and watch, with no account needed: go to paleontraining.com/preview/ai-machine-learning-fundamentals.

Q9

Is the course self-paced?

Yes. You can start as soon as your payment is confirmed and work through the lessons at your own pace.

Q10

How long do I keep access?

For life. Once your payment is confirmed you keep access to every lesson and video, with no expiry.

What drives the cost

Certification, depth, and format

The biggest cost driver is whether a course includes formal international certification, like a NEBOSH qualification — certification bodies charge separately from training providers, which is why a course bundling formal certification tends to cost meaningfully more than a practical, foundational course alone.

Beyond that, course depth and format matter: a structured, project-based course with real instruction and a capstone project (Paleon's own approach) differs in cost basis from a self-paced, video-only course. Any "free HSE certification" claim should be checked carefully for what's genuinely free versus what has hidden costs (exam fees, certification fees) not covered by the free portion.

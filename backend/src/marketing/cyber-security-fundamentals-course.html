Overview

What is the Cyber Security Fundamentals course?

In short

Cyber Security Fundamentals is a beginner-level online course from Paleon Training: 18 days of curriculum and 36 lessons. Each lesson is written, illustrated and paired with a video, with assignments and quizzes along the way. The course ends in a capstone project. The course fee is ₦200,000.

Who it’s for

Beginners: graduates, NYSC members and working professionals moving into IT security, SOC, IT/OT or governance, risk and compliance (GRC) roles. No prior security experience is needed; comfort using a computer is enough.

Curriculum

What you’ll learn

DayModule
Day 1Cyber Threats & Vulnerabilities
What cybersecurity protects and from whom; vulnerability management and thinking in risk.
Day 2Password & Authentication Security
How accounts get compromised; MFA; authentication vs. authorisation.
Day 3How Networks Work
The OSI model, TCP/IP and network segmentation.
Day 4Network Security: Firewalls, Monitoring & Secure Access
Firewalls, IDS/IPS, DDoS, VPNs and network monitoring.
Day 5Phishing & Social Engineering
Phishing, business email compromise, smishing and vishing.
Day 6Malware & Ransomware
Malware types and endpoint protection; ransomware response across three sectors.
Day 7Basic Cybersecurity Risk Management
Asset identification, risk assessment, risk registers and third-party risk.
Day 8Access Control & Identity Management
Least privilege, role-based access, privileged accounts and insider threats.
Day 9Incident Response
The six phases of incident response; detection and classification.
Day 10Security Awareness
Everyday cyber hygiene, reporting, remote work and physical security.
Day 11Cloud Security Fundamentals
IaaS, PaaS and SaaS; the shared responsibility model and cloud IAM.
Day 12Data Protection
Data classification, encryption and breach notification.
Days 13–14Oil & Gas pathway: IT/OT security
SCADA and industrial control systems, contractor access, pipeline security and OT risk.
Days 15–16Banking pathway
Online and mobile banking threats, payment-system security, fraud and customer data.
Days 17–18Telecoms pathway
Mobile network security, SIM fraud, availability, insider risk and 5G fundamentals.

How it’s taught

Practical, project-based learning

  • Illustrated lessons — every lesson has a diagram built for its specific concept.
  • A video for every lesson — read it, watch it, or both.
  • Assignments and quizzes to practise and check understanding as you go.
  • Capstone project: Sector Cybersecurity Risk Assessment & Incident Response Capstone — a risk assessment and incident response plan for an organisation in your chosen sector.
  • Certificate of completion when you finish the course.

This course includes three sector pathways. For how cybersecurity applies in energy specifically, see cybersecurity careers in oil & gas.

Common questions

Frequently asked

Q1

Do I need an IT background for Cyber Security Fundamentals?

No. The course is at beginner level and starts from how threats, accounts and networks work. Comfort using a computer and the internet is enough to begin.

Q2

Does the course cover all three sector pathways?

Yes. Days 1–12 cover core security, then Days 13–18 cover Oil & Gas, Banking and Telecommunications in two days each.

Q3

What do I produce at the end?

A capstone project: a sector cybersecurity risk assessment and incident response plan you can show to employers.

Q4

How is the course delivered?

Online, through the Paleon learning portal. Every lesson has written content with a custom illustration and a matching video, plus assignments and quizzes.

Q5

Does completing the course guarantee a job?

No. The course builds practical skills and a portfolio project; hiring decisions are made by employers.

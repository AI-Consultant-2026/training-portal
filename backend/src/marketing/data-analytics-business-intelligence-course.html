Overview

What is the Data Analytics & Business Intelligence course?

In short

Data Analytics & Business Intelligence is a beginner-level online course from Paleon Training: 12 days of curriculum and 24 lessons. Each lesson has two parts, written in plain English and paired with a video for each part, with a practical exercise and an eight-question knowledge check in every lesson. The course ends with your own data project and a 30-question final assessment. The course fee is ₦250,000.

Who it’s for

Graduates, NYSC members, students and working professionals from any background who want to work with data, whether you are aiming for a data analyst, reporting or business intelligence role, or want to build dashboards and reports in banking, telecoms, oil & gas, business or the public sector. No data, coding or maths background is needed.

Curriculum

What you’ll learn

DayModule
Day 1Introduction to Data Analytics, Data Types and Data Quality
Analytics vs business intelligence, the analysis process, types of data, and data quality and privacy.
Day 2Microsoft Excel for Data Analysis
Your first formulas, cleaning duplicates and blanks, IF, SUMIFS and XLOOKUP, and PivotTables.
Day 3Databases and SQL for Data Analysis
Building a database in DB Browser for SQLite; SELECT, WHERE and ORDER BY; GROUP BY and JOINs.
Day 4Introduction to Statistics for Data Analytics
Mean, median and mode; spread; percentages and growth rates; correlation and misleading conclusions.
Day 5Data Visualisation and Storytelling
Choosing the right chart, designing clear and honest charts, dashboard layout and turning numbers into a message.
Day 6Introduction to Power BI
How Power BI is organised, getting data in, cleaning it with Power Query and building your first report.
Day 7Power BI Data Modelling and DAX
Fact and dimension tables, relationships and the star schema, measures, CALCULATE and time intelligence.
Day 8Building Interactive Power BI Dashboards
Planning around business questions; slicers, filters and drill-down; KPI cards and sharing.
Day 9Python for Data Analysis
Python in Google Colab, loading a dataset with pandas, filtering, grouping and summarising, and a full exploratory analysis.
Day 10Business Analysis, Reporting and Decision-Making
Business questions and KPIs, clear analytical reports, recommendations and presenting to managers.
Day 11Data Analytics in Oil & Gas, Banking and Telecommunications
Production and downtime with an operations dashboard, unusual banking transactions, and telecoms customer churn.
Day 12Final Project and Assessment
Plan and build your own analysis of retail, oil & gas, banking or telecoms data, with a dashboard, report and presentation, then take the 30-question final assessment.

How it’s taught

Practical, project-based learning

  • Plain-English lessons — every idea starts with an everyday example before any technical word appears.
  • Two-part lessons with a video for each part — read it, watch it, or both, and stop between the parts if you need to.
  • Real tools, free versions: Excel for the web, SQL in DB Browser for SQLite, Power BI Desktop, and Python in Google Colab.
  • Practice datasets for fictional retail, oil & gas, banking and telecoms organisations, ready to download.
  • A practical exercise and an eight-question knowledge check in every lesson: clean a dataset, write SQL queries, build a PivotTable report, model data in Power BI, run an exploratory analysis in Python and more.
  • Your own project and a final assessment: plan and build an analysis of retail, oil & gas, banking or telecoms data with a Power BI dashboard, a short report and a presentation, then take a 30-question final assessment (pass mark 80%).
  • Certificate of completion when you finish the course.
  • CV and reference support when you finish the course.

Common questions

Frequently asked

Q1

Do I need experience with data, coding or maths?

No. Every idea is explained in plain English with an everyday example first, and each tool is introduced one small step at a time. If you can use a phone and a web browser, you can start.

Q2

What equipment do I need?

A laptop or desktop with a web browser and an internet connection, plus a free Microsoft account for Excel for the web and a free Google account for Colab. You can read and watch lessons on your phone, but the hands-on work is much easier on a computer. Power BI Desktop runs on Windows; the lessons show Mac users other ways to do the Power BI work.

Q3

Which tools will I use?

Excel for the web, DB Browser for SQLite and SQL, Power BI Desktop, and Python with pandas and Matplotlib in Google Colab. All have free versions. Practice datasets are provided for download in the lessons.

Q4

What do I produce at the end?

A practical exercise in every lesson (cleaning a dataset, SQL queries, a PivotTable report, a Power BI dashboard, an exploratory analysis in Python and more), and on the last day your own analysis of retail, oil & gas, banking or telecoms data, with a dashboard, a short report and a presentation for your portfolio. The course finishes with a 30-question final assessment (pass mark 80%).

Q5

Do you help with CVs and job applications?

Yes. Once you’ve completed the course, we help you prepare a CV that presents your skills and your project work as practical evidence for employers. We also provide a reference from Paleon Training confirming the course you completed. Email hello@paleontraining.com to request them.

Q6

Is this a recognised data analytics certification?

No. You receive a Paleon certificate of completion. It is not a university degree or a vendor certification such as Microsoft’s Power BI Data Analyst exam, though the course is a practical foundation for it.

Q7

Does completing the course guarantee a job?

No. The course builds practical skills and a portfolio, and we help with your CV and a reference once you finish. Hiring decisions are made by employers.

Q8

Can I try the course before paying?

Yes. The first lesson of Day 1 is free to read and watch, with no account needed: go to paleontraining.com/preview/data-analytics-business-intelligence.

Q9

Is the course self-paced?

Yes. You can start as soon as your payment is confirmed and work through the lessons at your own pace.

Q10

How long do I keep access?

For life. Once your payment is confirmed you keep access to every lesson and video, with no expiry.

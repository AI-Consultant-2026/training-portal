Overview

Every year, Nigerian universities send thousands of qualified graduates into a labour market where a degree alone often isn't the differentiator it used to be. Paleon Training partners with universities to help close that gap — giving final-year students and graduates practical, project-based digital skills that complement, not replace, their academic qualifications.

This page explains what a partnership includes, what we ask of your institution in return, and how to start the conversation.

For your university

What a Paleon partnership includes

We keep this genuinely low-cost and low-friction for the institution. Depending on what fits your students best, a partnership can include:

01

A free skills-readiness talk

A no-obligation session for your final-year cohort on what employers in Oil & Gas, Banking and Telecommunications actually look for beyond a degree.

02

A discounted group rate

Students who want to go further than the talk can enrol in a full course at a reduced group rate, arranged directly with your career services team.

03

A capstone showcase

Standout student capstone projects can be showcased — with your institution's permission — giving your strongest students visible proof of practical ability.

04

Outcome reporting

Aggregate, anonymised data on how your students engaged and what they built — useful for your own employability reporting, at no extra effort on your part.

05

A university partner listing

Your institution's name and logo on this page as an official Paleon Training university partner, plus mentions in our own outreach and social content where relevant.

What we ask in return

A fair, lightweight exchange

In return, we'd ask for the chance to present to your final-year classes, and — where appropriate — an introduction to your career services team or alumni network. That's the full extent of what we request to get started; everything else is arranged directly with you as the partnership develops.

How partnerships work

Two ways to partner

Most universities start as a Partner — the skills talk, the discounted group rate, and the partner listing above, with no ongoing commitment beyond that. For a smaller number of institutions, we're open to exploring a deeper Founding Partner relationship — closer involvement in capstone projects, and a conversation about a small number of sponsored seats — agreed individually, not offered as a standard package.

Common questions

Frequently asked

Q1

Does it cost our university anything to partner with Paleon Training?

No. The core partnership — a free skills-readiness talk, a discounted group rate, and a university partner listing — is free to the institution. A deeper founding-partner arrangement is scoped and agreed with you directly before anything is committed.

Q2

Does a Paleon Training course guarantee our graduates a job?

No, and we're careful never to make that claim. Our courses give students demonstrable, practical skills that complement their degree — genuine employability support, not a guaranteed outcome.

Q3

How is our university listed as a partner?

Once a partnership is agreed, we add your university's name and logo, with permission, to this page, and reference the partnership in our own outreach and social content where appropriate.

Q4

Who do we contact to start a partnership conversation?

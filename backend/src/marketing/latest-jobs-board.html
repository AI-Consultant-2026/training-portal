Before you apply

Scam warning

Real employers never charge application, test, training or medical fees. Ignore any “recruitment” message that asks you to pay, and never send your BVN, bank PIN or passwords.

Confirm on the employer’s page

Jobs are collected from public job boards and employers’ career pages. Check the deadline and details on the employer’s own site before you apply. Deadlines marked “Not stated” can close without notice.

We don’t recruit for these jobs

Paleon Training is not the employer and is not affiliated with any company listed. We list the jobs, and our courses build the skills they ask for. We can’t promise interviews or jobs.

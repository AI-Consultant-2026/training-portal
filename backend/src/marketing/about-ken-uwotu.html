Overview

Ken Uwotu is the Founder and technology visionary behind Paleon Training, a digital skills and career-development organisation focused on helping Nigerian students, graduates and non-graduates develop practical skills for today's changing job market.

Based in the United Kingdom, Ken brings together business, technology, digital education and management experience to develop Paleon Training as a platform connecting digital skills with real-world career and employment opportunities.

He holds a Master's Degree in Business Information Technology (Distinction) from London South Bank University, giving him a strong foundation in the relationship between technology, business systems, digital transformation and organisational development.

With more than 15 years of management experience, Ken has developed a practical understanding of how people, organisations, technology and business processes need to work together to achieve meaningful results.

Technology leadership

Leading Paleon Training's Technology Development

Ken leads a team of developers and digital professionals working across the United Kingdom and Nigeria.

Together, the team is developing the technology and digital infrastructure behind Paleon Training, including its online learning environment, course systems, assessments, quizzes, assignments and learner-management functionality.

Ken's responsibilities include overseeing:

  • Digital platform development
  • Learning Management System (LMS) development
  • Course and curriculum structures
  • Online lessons and assessments
  • Quizzes and practical assignments
  • Learner progression systems
  • Digital administration
  • Payment and registration systems
  • Technology infrastructure
  • Developer coordination across the UK and Nigeria
  • Continuous improvement of the Paleon Training platform

His approach is based on using technology to make learning practical, measurable and connected to career development.

Vision

Ken Uwotu's Vision for Paleon Training

At the heart of Ken's vision is a straightforward objective:

Bridge the gap between education and employment.

Paleon Training is being developed to help Nigerian learners move beyond simply gaining certificates. Ken wants learners to develop practical digital skills that they can demonstrate, apply and continue developing throughout their careers.

The model is built around:

Learn→ Practise→ Demonstrate→ Certify→ Connect to Opportunities

This means learners should not simply complete online lessons. They should have opportunities to test their knowledge, complete practical assignments and demonstrate their understanding of how digital skills are used in real-world environments.

Workforce focus

Developing Digital Skills for Nigeria's Future Workforce

Ken believes that digital skills will play an increasingly important role in Nigeria's workforce and economic development.

Paleon Training therefore focuses on practical training areas including:

Cyber Security Fundamentals GIS & Drone Mapping Renewable Energy Digital Systems Digital Marketing Social Media Management & Content Creation Health, Safety & Environment (HSE) Fundamentals

The training is designed with applications across industries such as: Oil & Gas • Banking • Telecommunications • Energy • Agriculture • Digital Services.

The objective is to help learners understand not only what they are learning, but also where those skills can be used and how they relate to potential career pathways.

UK & Nigeria

Connecting UK Expertise with Nigerian Talent

An important part of Ken's vision is building a meaningful technology and education connection between the United Kingdom and Nigeria.

Paleon Training's development model brings together professionals working across both countries. Ken's role is to help coordinate this collaboration and ensure that technology development, digital learning and the organisation's wider objectives remain aligned.

The aim is to combine UK-based technology and business experience with an understanding of Nigeria's education, employment and skills-development environment.

Career Intelligence

From Digital Training to Career Intelligence

Ken's ambition for Paleon Training extends beyond traditional online training.

He is developing the organisation around the idea of Career Intelligence — helping learners understand the relationship between their skills, industries, employers, job opportunities and career development.

This means providing learners with information and guidance around questions such as:

  • Which digital skills are employers looking for?
  • What skills are relevant to Oil & Gas?
  • Which digital skills can support careers in banking?
  • What technology skills are relevant to telecommunications?
  • What courses can graduates take after NYSC?
  • How can a learner build practical experience?
  • How can digital skills strengthen a graduate's career prospects?

The long-term vision is to create an ecosystem where learning, practical skills, career information and employment opportunities are connected.

Philosophy

More Than a Certificate

Ken's philosophy is that completing a course should be the beginning of a learner's development, not the end.

His vision is for Paleon Training to help learners move through a progression:

01

Knowledge

Understanding the principles behind a digital skill.

02

Practice

Applying that knowledge through practical activities.

03

Evidence

Demonstrating what the learner can actually do.

04

Certification

Receiving recognition for completing the required learning.

05

Career Development

Understanding how those skills can be applied to real-world opportunities.

This approach reflects Ken's belief that employability requires more than qualifications alone.

The initiative

A UK–Nigeria Digital Skills Initiative

Under Ken's leadership, Paleon Training is being developed as a UK–Nigeria digital skills initiative, with technology development and collaboration taking place across both countries.

The organisation's wider ambition is to work with learners, universities, career centres, employers, industry partners and other organisations to create stronger connections between education and the workplace.

For Nigerian students, graduates and people who did not attend university, this means creating additional pathways into digital learning and skills development.

The long view

Ken Uwotu's Long-Term Goal

Ken's long-term goal is to help build a generation of Nigerian learners who are not only qualified, but digitally capable, practically experienced and better prepared for the modern workplace.

His vision for Paleon Training is therefore much bigger than delivering online courses.

It is about building a platform where a learner can:

Discover a skill→ Learn the skill→ Practise the skill→ Demonstrate the skill→ Understand its career value→ Connect it to opportunity

That is the foundation of Ken Uwotu's vision for Paleon Training.

Overview

What is the Digital Marketing course?

In short

Digital Marketing is a beginner-level online course from Paleon Training: 8 days of curriculum and 16 lessons. Each lesson is written, illustrated and paired with a video, with assignments and quizzes along the way. The course ends in a capstone project. The course fee is ₦150,000.

Who it’s for

Beginners, including graduates, NYSC members, business owners and staff in marketing, sales or communications roles who want structured, practical digital marketing skills. No prior marketing experience is needed.

Curriculum

What you’ll learn

DayModule
Day 1Digital Marketing Fundamentals & Strategy
The digital marketing funnel and an overview of channels.
Day 2Audience Research & Channel Strategy
Buyer personas, competitive analysis and building a channel strategy.
Day 3On-Page, Off-Page & Local SEO
How search engines work; on-page, technical, off-page and local SEO.
Day 4Content Marketing & Email Campaigns
Content and lead magnets; email list building, segmentation and deliverability.
Day 5Google Ads: Structure, Bidding & Ad Copy
Campaign types, Quality Score, structure, budgeting and conversion tracking.
Day 6Analytics, Attribution & Marketing ROI
Google Analytics, attribution models, dashboards, ROI, CAC and LTV.
Day 7E-commerce Marketing & Automation
E-commerce and cart abandonment; automation, CRM and journey mapping.
Day 8Integrated Strategy & Capstone Planning
Integrated campaigns, budget allocation, emerging trends and capstone planning.

How it’s taught

Practical, project-based learning

  • Illustrated lessons — every lesson has a diagram built for its specific concept.
  • A video for every lesson — read it, watch it, or both.
  • Assignments and quizzes to practise and check understanding as you go.
  • Capstone project: An integrated digital marketing strategy capstone that brings the channels together into one plan.
  • Certificate of completion when you finish the course.

Common questions

Frequently asked

Q1

Is the Digital Marketing course suitable for beginners?

Yes. It starts with the marketing funnel and channel strategy before moving to SEO, ads, analytics and automation.

Q2

Does the course cover Google Ads and SEO?

Yes. Day 3 covers on-page, off-page, technical and local SEO, and Day 5 covers Google Ads structure, bidding, ad copy and conversion tracking.

Q3

What do I produce at the end?

An integrated digital marketing strategy capstone you can use as portfolio evidence.

Q4

How is the course delivered?

Online, through the Paleon learning portal. Every lesson has written content with a custom illustration and a matching video, plus assignments and quizzes.

Q5

Does completing the course guarantee a job?

No. The course builds practical skills and a portfolio project; hiring decisions are made by employers.

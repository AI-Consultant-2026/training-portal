How it works

Three steps

01

Share your code

Every student with a Paleon account gets a personal referral code and invite link. Log in and open your referral page, then send them to friends, coursemates or your NYSC set, on WhatsApp or anywhere else.

02

They join and pay

Your friend enters your code when they register (your invite link fills it in for them), then enrols and pays for any Paleon course.

03

You both get rewarded

Once their first course payment is confirmed, you earn {{REFERRER_REWARD}} and your friend earns a {{REFEREE_REWARD}} welcome reward. Each of you chooses airtime, a data bundle or credit towards a Paleon course.

The rules

Good to know

  • Self-referrals and duplicate accounts don't qualify.
  • A referral counts once, on the friend's first confirmed course payment.
  • Codes are entered at registration, so share yours before your friend signs up.
  • Paleon Training builds skills and employability; it doesn't guarantee a job, so please don't promise one when you share.
  • We may adjust or end the programme at any time.

Common questions

Frequently asked

Q1

Who can refer friends to Paleon Training?

Any student with a Paleon Training account. Log in and open your referral page to get your personal code and invite link.

Q2

How much do I earn for each referral?

You earn {{REFERRER_REWARD}} for every friend who joins with your code and pays for a course. There's no limit on how many friends you can refer.

Q3

What does my friend get?

Your friend earns a {{REFEREE_REWARD}} welcome reward once their first course payment is confirmed, as airtime, a data bundle or course credit.

Q4

When are rewards paid?

After the referred friend's first course payment is confirmed. The Paleon team issues rewards by hand, so allow a few working days for airtime or data.

Q5

Can I refer myself or use a second account?

No. Self-referrals and duplicate accounts don't qualify.
